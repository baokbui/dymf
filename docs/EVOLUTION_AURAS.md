# Evolution aura presentation ✅

Planted Epic+ Brainrots use rising flame columns and sparse rising sparkles,
with a rarity outline and faint body tint. Colors follow the highest claimed
tier: Epic violet, Legendary gold, Mythic red-orange, Immortal crimson and God
rainbow. The earlier halo, energy shell and point-source spark designs are removed.
Evolution eligibility, stats, combat, placement and saving are unchanged.

## Ownership and construction

`BrainrotService.EvolutionAura` authors five replicated ParticleEmitters:
CoreFlames, OuterFlames, Motes, Glints and BurstFlames. BrainrotFeedback retains
its aura API, replacement/cleanup and Stoneheart appearance ownership.
`EvolutionVfxConfig` owns presentation tuning; `AuraQuality` changes client-local
rates and TimeScale without changing simulation or server animation.

`AuraBounds.measure` combines visible-part boxes and padded transformed Bone
positions. This includes skinned feet and wide limbs without changing
ModelUtil placement or feet snapping. The invisible, massless, noncolliding
AuraVfx carrier spans these bounds. A second welded Part, FlameVolume, begins
at the same bottom but has a lowered ceiling. All five emitters use this same
Box/Volume source. There are no leg/torso attachments or split emitter pairs.

The source ceiling accounts for maximum flame travel and estimated stretched
sprite radius, allowing 12% model-height headroom (minimum 0.6 studs). The inset
is capped to retain at least 25% of source height on small models. This is a
presentation envelope estimate, not a pixel-exact clipping plane. No additional
lights, physical studs, rings or per-unit animation loops are created.

## Current motion and density

Flames retain size 2.2, vertical squash -0.65, bright inner cores and saturated
outer color. They rise at 12–18% of tuned height per second with 1% height
acceleration and live 0.6–0.95 seconds, fading completely before replenishment.

Motes and Glints start throughout FlameVolume and rise at 40–60% of tuned
height per second, with 35-degree sideways spread, drag 0.1 and upward
acceleration of 4% height per second squared. Tuned height is model height
clamped to 3–12 studs. Motes live 1.3–1.8 seconds and Glints 1.2–1.7 seconds.
They fade while rising and are not locked to the model. Sparks use the exact
evolution color (God rainbow), brightness 3.6 and a small depth offset for
visibility against flames. Flame brightness is 4.8 core / 3.6 outer.

## Viewer quality and budgets

| Effects setting | Roblox SavedQualityLevel | Aura band |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | 8–10 | High |
| High | 4–7 | Medium |
| High | 1–3 | Low |
| High | Automatic / unavailable | Medium |
| Low | Any | Low |
| Off | Any | Off |

Automatic uses a conservative Medium fallback, not a live device-performance
measurement. Saved graphics changes trigger refreshes without restarting.

| Band | Maximum particle-visible units | Maximum detailed local units | Maximum purchase bursts per refresh |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| High | 28 | 8 | 4 |
| Medium | 18 | 6 | 3 |
| Low | 10 | 0 | 2 |
| Off | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Nearest units within 140 studs receive visible slots. Detailed slots are local
plot units within 70 studs; other visible units use Low. Low retains core flames
and sparse sparks. Hidden, distant, over-budget and Off units retain their
outline/faint tint without aura particles. Other Plots controls suppress other
plots' auras. DayPaused freezes particle TimeScale, including resume countdown;
Stoneheart suppresses emission while retaining stone appearance ownership.

One half-second controller loop handles distance/crowd changes. Settings, tags,
attributes and descendant events handle other refreshes. Emitters are cached,
unchanged rates are not restarted, and removal/destruction disconnects watchers.

## Purchase celebration

An accepted evolution purchase passes the optional celebration flag to
BrainrotService.applyEvolutionStats. Transient model EvolutionBurst and carrier
BurstId serials match one local burst after replication. Placement, restoration
and developer roster application do not celebrate; nothing is persisted.

Burst counts per layer on High/Medium/Low are CoreFlames 10/6/3,
OuterFlames 6/3/0, Motes 14/8/4, Glints 8/4/2 and BurstFlames 4/2/1.
Suppressed or over-budget bursts never replay when visibility returns. No new
remote or reward/save path is introduced.

## Verification and remaining QA

EvolutionAura.spec covers five tiers, shared-volume spark sources, meaningful
rise distance, rate budgets, flame headroom estimate, physics and unchanged rigs.
AuraBounds.spec covers skinned feet below mesh boxes, wide limbs and helper
exclusion. AuraQuality.spec covers 35 units, quality bands, stable playback,
distance/crowd/burst caps, serial matching, pause/stone, hiding and cleanup.
These fixtures and Rojo build pass without accessing real saves or DevConfig.

Earlier isolated visual probes verified material/sprite loading and identified
Top-face Surface emission as a problem. Their leg-anchor screenshots do not
verify the current shared-volume revision. Current in-play appearance, mobile,
full combat and four-player performance QA remain 🔜. Do not infer frame-time
or visual acceptance from property assertions alone.
